Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Overview

Arman Design Lab ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as when you fill out our contact form. This may include:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your business name
  • Details about the project or service you're enquiring about

We may also collect limited technical information automatically, such as browser type and general usage patterns, to help us understand how the site is used.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to enquiries, discuss potential projects, and, where applicable, deliver services you've requested. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies

This website may use minimal cookies to support basic functionality and to understand general traffic patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party tools — such as email or analytics providers — to help operate this website. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function.

6. Data Retention

We retain enquiry information only as long as necessary to respond to you or fulfill an ongoing engagement, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us directly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please reach out at hello@armandesignlab.com.
