How We Work

Seven stages. No guesswork, no scope creep.

Every project follows the same disciplined sequence — so you always know what's happening, and what comes next.

01

Discovery

A focused conversation about your business, your customers, and what success looks like — the foundation everything else is built on.

02

Research

We study your industry, your competitors, and your customers' expectations to find the angle that makes your site stand apart.

03

Design

A complete visual direction — palette, typography, layout — presented for your review before any code is written.

04

Development

Clean, semantic, fast-loading code that brings the approved design to life, responsive across every device.

05

Testing

Every page, form, link, and interaction is checked across browsers and screen sizes before it reaches your customers.

06

Launch

Your site goes live — domain, hosting, and analytics configured, confirmed working, and handed over to you.

07

Support

We remain available for updates, questions, and small changes as your business — and your website — continues to grow.

Timeline

Most projects launch in three to five weeks.

Timelines vary by scope — a landing page can be ready in days, while a full custom build typically takes four to five weeks from discovery to launch.
